What Is Substance Abuse Counseling?

Substance abuse counseling is evidence-based treatment for substance use and co-occurring disorders. I offer it in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina. Care follows the ASAM criteria, so the level of care matches what you need. I offer individual, group, and family sessions.

My name is Dr. Chris Coleman. I am a licensed substance abuse treatment provider, a Master Addiction Counselor, and a DOT Substance Abuse Professional, with more than 20 years in this field. I have worked in jails, drug court, community services boards, and inpatient and outpatient programs. I know recovery from many sides. That matters here.

Common questions

Substance Abuse Counseling FAQs

What is substance use disorder treatment?

Substance use disorder treatment is evidence-based care for problems with alcohol or drugs, often alongside mental health support. It can include individual counseling, group sessions, family work, and assessment for the right level of care.

How does outpatient addiction counseling work?

Outpatient counseling lets you live at home while you attend regular sessions. I use the ASAM criteria to set how often sessions happen and adjust as your recovery changes.

What are co-occurring disorders?

Co-occurring disorders are when a substance use disorder and a mental health condition, like anxiety, depression, or trauma, happen together. Treating both at once gives recovery a stronger footing.

Can I do substance abuse counseling online in Virginia?

Yes. Substance abuse counseling is available in person in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina. Some services, like in-office drug screens, are in person only.
