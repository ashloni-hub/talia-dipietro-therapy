What Is Trauma Therapy?

Trauma therapy is evidence-based care that helps you process painful experiences so they lose their grip on the present. I provide trauma therapy in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina.

I am Dr. Chris Coleman, a Certified Clinical Trauma Professional and a licensed counselor with more than 20 years in this field. I draw on trauma-informed care and cognitive processing approaches. The work goes at a pace your nervous system can handle. Faith is offered here, never forced.

Common questions

Trauma Therapy FAQs

What is trauma-informed therapy?

Trauma-informed therapy is care that recognizes how trauma affects the mind and body and keeps you safe and in control throughout. It focuses on stability first, then processing painful experiences at a pace you can handle.

How do I know if I have PTSD?

Common signs include intrusive memories, avoidance, feeling on edge, and changes in mood or sleep after a traumatic event. A professional assessment can tell you more, and I offer one.

What does trauma counseling involve?

Trauma counseling usually starts with building safety and coping skills, then gently processing the experiences that still affect you. I use trauma-informed care and cognitive processing approaches.

Can trauma therapy be done online?

Yes. Trauma therapy is available in person in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina.
