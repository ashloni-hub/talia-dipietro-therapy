Certifications & credentials
Meet Dr. Chris Coleman

Faith-Based Counselor & Addiction Specialist

I hold a PhD in Christian counseling, and I am an ordained minister. I am a licensed professional counselor and licensed substance abuse treatment provider in Virginia, with licensure in Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina.

My certifications include Master Addiction Counselor, Certified Clinical Trauma Professional, and DOT Substance Abuse Professional. I entered this field in 1994, and for more than 20 years I have treated substance use, co-occurring, and mental health disorders across jails, drug court, community services boards, and inpatient and outpatient programs.

You are treated with dignity and respect as a person, not a diagnosis.
