the basics

What Is Christian Counseling?

Christian counseling is faith-based counseling that joins biblical principles with evidence-based clinical care. I offer it in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina. You do not need to be of the Christian faith to be here. I will never force my beliefs on you.

My name is Dr. Chris Coleman. I hold a PhD in Christian counseling, I am an ordained minister, and I am a licensed professional counselor and substance abuse treatment provider with more than 20 years in this field. I will ask permission before I pray with you. I will always pray for you. I meet you as a person, not a diagnosis.

Common questions

Christian Counseling FAQs

What is Christian counseling?

Christian counseling is faith-based counseling that joins biblical principles with evidence-based clinical care. It welcomes your faith into the room while using proven clinical approaches.

How is Christian counseling different from regular counseling?

The clinical skill is the same, and the difference is that faith is welcomed as part of your care. I integrate a biblical worldview with evidence-based methods, and I follow your lead on how much faith to include.

Do I have to be a Christian to receive faith-based counseling?

No. You do not need to be of the Christian faith to receive services, and beliefs are never forced. I will ask permission before I pray with you, and I will always respect where you stand.

Can Christian counseling be done online?

Yes. Christian counseling is available in person in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina.

Do you take insurance?

Yes. Insurance is accepted through Headway, including Aetna, Cigna, Anthem/BCBS, United Health/Optum, Carefirst, Carelon Behavioral Health, Sentara Health, and some Kaiser plans, along with private pay.
