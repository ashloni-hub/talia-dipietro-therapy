What Is a DUI Assessment?

A DUI assessment is a professional substance use evaluation that reviews your history with alcohol and drugs and recommends the right level of care. I provide DUI and substance use assessments in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina.

I am Dr. Chris Coleman, a licensed substance abuse treatment provider and a DOT-qualified Substance Abuse Professional, with more than 20 years in addiction assessment and treatment. The assessment is evidence-based and biopsychosocial. You leave with a clear recommendation. That is the goal. I keep it honest. You do not need to be of the Christian faith to be here, and faith is offered, never forced.

please note

What I Do and Do Not Do

I provide assessments and DOT SAP evaluations, and I give you a clear written recommendation. I do not testify, appear in court, or complete evaluations to satisfy a court, judge, or attorney. I do not provide fit-for-duty, return-to-work, medical, FMLA, workers compensation, military, or SSI or SSDI evaluations. If your case is driven only by court or attorney requirements, I will refer you to a provider who does that work.

Common questions

DUI Assessment & DOT SAP FAQs

What is a DOT SAP evaluation?

A DOT SAP evaluation is a face-to-face assessment for safety-sensitive employees who violated Department of Transportation drug or alcohol rules. As a qualified Substance Abuse Professional, I evaluate you and recommend the education or treatment the return-to-duty process requires.

Do I need a substance use assessment after a DUI?

Often, yes. Courts, employers, and licensing boards frequently ask for one after a DUI. I provide the assessment and a written recommendation, though I do not appear in court or complete court-satisfaction paperwork.

What happens in a DUI assessment?

You complete an evidence-based, biopsychosocial assessment of your alcohol and drug history. I review it with you and recommend the right level of care.

How long does a SAP evaluation take?

A SAP evaluation is usually completed in one comprehensive session, with follow-up as the return-to-duty process requires.

Do you take insurance for assessments?

DOT SAP evaluations and the initial comprehensive assessment are private-pay and not billed to insurance. Some substance use sessions can use insurance through Headway.
