the basics

Counseling for Those Who Serve

I provide counseling for military members, veterans, first responders, and nurses in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina. Care focuses on trauma, PTSD, job stress, and the toll of carrying other people’s worst days.

I am Dr. Chris Coleman, a Certified Clinical Trauma Professional and licensed counselor with more than 20 years in trauma and substance use. What you say here stays here, within the limits of the law. You do not need to be of the Christian faith to be here. Faith is offered, never forced.

please note

What This Is, and What It Is Not

I provide confidential counseling and trauma care. I do not perform fit-for-duty, return-to-work, or disability evaluations. I do not complete SSI, SSDI, workers compensation, or military evaluation paperwork. If you need a fitness or disability evaluation, I will point you toward a provider who does that work.

how it works

How I Approach Your Care

I get the weight of the work. You will not have to explain what the job does to a person.

Confidential. What you share stays private, within the limits of the law, and records go out only with a release you sign.

Trauma-informed and evidence-based. I use trauma-informed care and cognitive processing approaches, at a pace you set.

Faith welcomed, not forced. Biblical principles are part of my approach when you want them, and never when you do not.

Honest and direct, with compassion. If it causes a problem, then it is a problem. I will tell you the truth and walk it with you.

Common questions

First Responder & Military Counseling FAQs

Do you offer counseling for first responders?

Yes. I provide confidential counseling for first responders, along with military members, veterans, and nurses, in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia.

What is counseling for military and veterans like?

It focuses on trauma, job and combat stress, and the transition home, at a pace you control. I use trauma-informed and evidence-based approaches.

Can nurses get confidential counseling?

Yes. Your sessions are confidential, within the limits of the law, and records are released only with a written authorization you sign.

How does trauma counseling help first responders?

Trauma counseling helps you process critical incidents so they stop intruding on sleep, focus, and relationships. It also builds coping skills for the next hard call.

Do you take insurance?

Yes. Insurance is accepted through Headway, including Aetna, Cigna, Anthem/BCBS, United Health/Optum, Carefirst, Carelon Behavioral Health, Sentara Health, and some Kaiser plans, along with private pay.
