the basics

What Is Family Counseling for Addiction?

Family counseling for addiction is support for the people who love someone with a substance use disorder. I provide it in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina. Addiction is a family experience. You did not cause it, and you cannot control it. You matter here too.

I am Dr. Chris Coleman, a licensed substance abuse treatment provider and counselor with more than 20 years walking families through recovery. You do not need to be of the Christian faith to be here. Faith is offered, never forced.

what we work on

Does This Sound Like You?

  • You are walking on eggshells, waiting for the next crisis
  • You have covered, bailed out, or made excuses, and it keeps happening
  • You have lost sleep, savings, or yourself trying to help

Common questions

Family Counseling for Addiction FAQs

How can family counseling help with a loved one's addiction?

Family counseling helps you set healthy boundaries, change patterns that keep everyone stuck, and support recovery without losing yourself. I work with individuals and families affected by a loved one’s substance use.

What is codependency?

Codependency is when your wellbeing gets so tied to another person that you lose sight of your own needs. In family counseling, those patterns get named, and healthier ones take their place.

How do I support someone in recovery?

You can encourage treatment, hold firm boundaries, and take care of yourself, without carrying their recovery for them. I will help you find that balance.

Can family recovery counseling be done online?

Yes. Family counseling is available in person in Ashland, VA and online across Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, Florida, Washington DC, and South Carolina.
